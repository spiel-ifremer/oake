# OAKE Governance

## Purpose

This document describes the governance model of the Ontology of the Astronomical Knowledge Ecosystem (OAKE).

OAKE is developed as an open scientific project whose objective is to build a sustainable semantic foundation for describing and connecting the worldwide astronomy ecosystem.

The governance model is intended to evolve progressively as the project grows and new contributors join the initiative.

---

## Current governance

At its current stage, OAKE is maintained by a single maintainer.

### Maintainer

**Steven Piel**

The maintainer is currently responsible for:

- defining the project vision;
- coordinating development activities;
- reviewing proposed changes;
- managing releases;
- ensuring the long-term scientific and semantic consistency of the project.

---

## Guiding principles

OAKE is developed according to the following principles:

- openness;
- scientific transparency;
- semantic interoperability;
- reuse of existing standards whenever appropriate;
- FAIR principles;
- community collaboration;
- long-term sustainability.

---

## Decision-making

At the current stage, decisions are made by the maintainer.

Whenever possible, technical and scientific decisions are expected to be discussed openly with contributors and documented transparently.

As the project matures, decision-making will progressively evolve towards a more collaborative governance model.

---

## Communities of interest

OAKE aims to foster collaboration across existing astronomy and Semantic Web communities.

The project intends to engage with researchers, engineers and practitioners involved in areas such as:

- ontology engineering;
- Semantic Web technologies;
- knowledge graphs;
- FAIR digital objects;
- Virtual Observatory standards;
- professional astronomy;
- amateur astronomy;
- citizen science;
- dark-sky monitoring;
- astronomy education and outreach.

As the project develops, representatives from these communities will be invited to contribute to both the technical development and the governance of OAKE.

---

## Contributions

Contributions from the community are welcome.

Future contributors may participate by:

- proposing new concepts and ontology modules;
- reviewing ontology design;
- contributing documentation;
- reporting issues;
- suggesting improvements;
- developing software tools;
- contributing knowledge graph content.

Contribution guidelines will be published in a future version of the project.

---

## Future governance

OAKE is intended to evolve from a maintainer-led project into a community-driven initiative.

The long-term objective is to establish an open governance model bringing together expertise from astronomy, ontology engineering, Semantic Web technologies and FAIR data.

As the project grows, the governance model may progressively include:

- multiple maintainers;
- domain editors;
- ontology reviewers;
- technical contributors;
- a scientific advisory board;
- a community steering committee.

The governance model will evolve progressively while preserving the project's scientific vision, openness, interoperability and long-term sustainability.

---

## Governance evolution

This governance document is itself part of the project and will evolve alongside OAKE.
